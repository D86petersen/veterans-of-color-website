You served. Now let us serve you.

If you are a woman veteran facing homelessness or housing instability, this page is for you. You deserve safety, dignity, and support — and you don't have to face this alone.

If you need help right now

Our Fresno shelter is still being secured — but help is available today through these free, confidential national resources, open 24/7:

  • Veterans Crisis Line 988, then press 1 — or text 838255
    Free, confidential support for veterans in crisis and those who love them.
  • National Call Center for Homeless Veterans 1-877-4AID-VET (1-877-424-3838)
    Connects homeless and at-risk veterans with VA housing and services.
  • Women Veterans Call Center 1-855-VA-WOMEN (1-855-829-6636)
    Answers questions about VA services and benefits for women veterans.

These are national resources operated by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and its partners — not by Veterans of Color. We list them because no woman should wait for help.

Who We Serve

Who qualifies

  • Women veterans ages 25–55 experiencing homelessness
  • Women veterans in housing crisis or at risk of homelessness
  • Serving Fresno and California's Central Valley, with an emphasis on women of color

All of our services are free — there is never a charge to the women we serve.

What to Expect

When our doors open

Here's what life at Veterans of Color will look like:

  1. Welcome & safety first
    A safe bed in a 24/7-staffed home, a warm meal, and time to breathe. No judgment — just welcome.
  2. Your care team
    A dedicated case manager learns your story and your goals, then builds a plan with you — housing, health, work, family, benefits.
  3. Wraparound support
    Trauma-informed counseling (including MST care), job training, GI Bill and VA benefits help, childcare, peer community — all under one roof.
  4. Your next chapter
    Transitional housing for 6–24 months, with our housing navigator working toward your permanent home — and 70% of residents reaching stable housing is our goal.

Our Fresno facility is being secured now. If you'd like to be notified when we begin accepting residents, send us a message — we'll keep your information private and contact you only about this.
