Programs & Services

Six wraparound programs under one roof — every service free to the women we serve, delivered with trauma-informed, culturally competent care.

1. Housing Services

A safe bed is the beginning, not the end. Our housing continuum meets each woman where she is and walks with her toward permanence.

  • Emergency shelter — 24/7 access for women in immediate crisis
  • Transitional housing — 6 to 24 months of stable residence with full services
  • Permanent supportive housing assistance — help securing long-term homes
  • Housing navigation & placement — a dedicated navigator working with landlords and housing authorities

2. Health & Wellness

Healing the whole person — body and mind — with care designed for the experiences women veterans carry.

  • Primary healthcare coordination — connecting residents to trusted providers
  • Mental health counseling — individual support from qualified counselors
  • PTSD & trauma-informed care — including specialized support for military sexual trauma (MST)
  • Substance use treatment programs — recovery support without judgment

3. Employment & Education

Translating military strength into civilian success — from first resume to first paycheck and beyond.

  • Job training & placement — skills development and employer connections
  • Resume building & interview prep — presenting service as the asset it is
  • Educational assistance & GI Bill navigation — making earned benefits work
  • Financial literacy workshops — budgeting, credit, and planning for independence

4. VA Benefits & Legal

No woman should lose the benefits she earned to paperwork. We make sure she doesn't.

  • VA benefits enrollment & advocacy — standing beside her through the process
  • Benefits claims support — help filing and following up on claims
  • Legal assistance referrals — connections to trusted legal aid
  • Document recovery services — replacing lost IDs, DD-214s, and vital records

5. Family Support

Many women veterans are mothers. Healing and housing must work for the whole family.

  • Childcare assistance — so mothers can work, study, and heal
  • Parenting classes — practical support for strong families
  • Family reunification services — rebuilding bonds where possible
  • Children's programs & activities — a safe, joyful place for kids too

6. Life Skills & Community

The sisterhood doesn't end at checkout. Community is the program that keeps working.

  • Peer support groups — healing alongside women who understand
  • Life skills training — everyday tools for independent living
  • Recreation & wellness activities — joy as part of recovery
  • Community integration support — roots in Fresno that last
The Environment

Designed for dignity

Our 30-bed Fresno facility will be staffed around the clock — a safe, welcoming home, not an institution. Every resident is paired with a case manager, every program is trauma-informed, and every service is free.

We are currently securing our facility. Founding supporters are making this home possible right now.
