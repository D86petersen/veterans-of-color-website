Our Mission

A safe, dignified pathway home

“To provide safe, supportive housing and comprehensive services that empower female veterans to transition from homelessness to stable, independent living while honoring their service and sacrifice.”

Women are the fastest-growing segment of the veteran homeless population, yet gender-specific housing for women veterans remains critically scarce. Veterans of Color answers that need in California's Central Valley — with trauma-informed care, wraparound services, and a community that understands the intersection of service, gender, and race.

Comprehensive Care

Six pillars. One roof.

Everything a woman veteran needs to rebuild her life — housing, health, work, benefits, family, and community — delivered together, free of charge.

Housing Services

24/7 emergency shelter, transitional housing for 6–24 months, and navigation to permanent supportive housing.

Health & Wellness

Primary care coordination, mental health counseling, and trauma-informed PTSD and MST care.

Employment & Education

Job training and placement, resume and interview prep, GI Bill navigation, and financial literacy.

VA Benefits & Legal

VA enrollment and advocacy, claims support, legal referrals, and document recovery.

Family Support

Childcare assistance, parenting classes, family reunification, and children's programs.

Life Skills & Community

Peer support groups, life-skills training, wellness activities, and community integration.
