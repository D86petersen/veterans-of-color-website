About Veterans of Color

A nonprofit organization dedicated to providing comprehensive shelter, support services, and transitional housing for female veterans experiencing homelessness or housing instability.

Our Story

Built for the women who served

Women are the fastest-growing segment of the veteran homeless population, and female veterans face homelessness at two to four times the rate of civilian women. Yet gender-specific housing designed for women veterans remains critically scarce — especially care that understands military sexual trauma and the intersection of race, gender, and service.

Veterans of Color was founded to close that gap in California's Central Valley: a comprehensive, trauma-informed shelter where women veterans — with an emphasis on women of color — find not just a bed, but a community, a care team, and a dignified pathway to stable, independent living.

We are live and in our building stages now, securing our Fresno home and assembling the founding community of donors, volunteers, and partners who will open our doors.

What Guides Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

“To provide safe, supportive housing and comprehensive services that empower female veterans to transition from homelessness to stable, independent living while honoring their service and sacrifice.”

Our Vision

“A future where no female veteran experiences homelessness, and all have access to the resources, dignity, and support needed to thrive in civilian life.”

Core Values

How we show up, every day

  • DignityTreating every veteran with respect and honor — always.
  • CommunityBuilding supportive relationships and peer networks that last beyond our walls.
  • EmpowermentProviding the tools and resources for genuine self-sufficiency.
  • ServiceContinuing the mission of service — now in service of those who served.
  • Holistic CareAddressing physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing together, never in isolation.
Governance & Team

Led with accountability

Veterans of Color operates as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, governed by a Board of Directors that brings together veterans, community leaders, healthcare professionals, and business executives. Our leadership team is being assembled as we move toward opening day:

  • Executive Director — organizational leadership and community partnerships
  • Program Director — coordination of all resident services
  • Case Managers (3) — individualized support for every resident
  • Housing Navigator — placement coordination and landlord partnerships
  • Overnight Staff (2) — 24/7 safety and coverage
  • Administrative Assistant — operations support

Our staff will be culturally competent and trained in trauma-informed care, with deep sensitivity to the needs of women veterans of color.

Year-One Goals

What success looks like

We hold ourselves to measurable outcomes from day one:

50+ women served

Welcoming more than fifty female veterans into shelter and transitional housing in our first year of operations.

70% to stable housing

Transitioning at least seven in ten residents into stable, permanent housing.

60% employed at exit

Supporting residents into jobs and careers so independence lasts.

Long term, we aim for 80% housing stability at six-month follow-up and expansion to a 50-bed facility by Year Three — building a model other communities can replicate.
